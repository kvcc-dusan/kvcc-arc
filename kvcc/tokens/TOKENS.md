## Focus

Pointer focus draws no ring. Keyboard focus (`:focus-visible`) draws one shared accent outline from `foundation.css`. Tune with `--focus-outline`, `--focus-outline-width` (2px), `--focus-outline-offset` (2px). Never write per-component outlines or box-shadow rings.
